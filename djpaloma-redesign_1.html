Get to Know DJ Paloma

A journey of faith, carried through music

DJ Paloma is one of Ghana's most sought-after Gospel DJs, known for creating praise and worship experiences that bring congregations and communities together in faith. Years of spinning at churches, conferences and national gospel events have shaped a signature style: contemporary gospel hits woven seamlessly with timeless worship classics.

Every set is built around one goal — creating a space where hearts are lifted and God is glorified.

Mission

To use music as a tool for worship — spreading the gospel message and creating faith-driven experiences that glorify God, one event at a time.
